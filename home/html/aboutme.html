Personal & Professional Background

I started McCallister Financial Labs after seeing firsthand how crucial financial alignment is early in marriage. My coaching model gives newly married couples a clear framework for combining money, building joint emergency funds, and establishing realistic budgets without feeling restricted.

Key Focus Areas:

  • Newlywed Budget Planning: Building joint cash-flow systems that foster communication, transparency, and financial teamwork.
  • Skiing & Outdoor Recreation: Staying active in the mountains during winter and planning regular trips with my wife.
  • Dividend Growth Strategies: Analyzing dividend growth assets to create passive income streams over multi-decade horizons.
  • Travel & Point Optimization: Leveraging credit card reward strategies to travel affordably within a structured budget.
